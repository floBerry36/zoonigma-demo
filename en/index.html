Where does this animal live?

One animal photo, one globe to spin: drop your pin where you think it lives. The closer you are to its range, the more points you score.

Play

How to play

  1. Look at the photo: the landscape is a clue.
  2. Open the world map, spin the globe and drop your pin.
  3. Confirm: the animal's range appears, with the distance and your points.

A game has 5 rounds, up to 5,000 points each.

Test prototype: 18 species, hand-drawn approximate ranges.
