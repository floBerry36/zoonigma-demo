Animals of the Globe

A photo, a globe: where does this animal live?

How to play
  1. Look at the photo: the landscape is a clue.
  2. Open the world map, spin the globe and drop your pin.
  3. Confirm: the animal's range appears, with the distance and your points.

A game has 5 rounds, up to 5,000 points each.
